Common questions

It's normal to wonder how this works

Is it normal to feel nervous about starting counseling?
Yes — completely. Most people feel a mix of anticipation and anxiety when reaching out. Those feelings don't mean you're not ready; they're a sign you're stepping into something new and meaningful.
What should I expect in the first session?
The first session is about getting to know each other — your story, what's bringing you in, and what you hope changes. There's no pressure to open up faster than feels right; trust comes first.
What do I talk about in counseling?
Anything that feels important to you. Some people arrive with a specific struggle; others just know life should feel better. There's no wrong topic — if it matters to you, it matters to me.
