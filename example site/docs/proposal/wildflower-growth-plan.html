Let's fill your practice from right here on the Shore.

Carolyn,

In Colorado, the phone rang. Referrals came in, groups filled, and you never had to wonder where the next client would come from. Then you moved your life and your practice to the Shore, and things got quiet. When you are the one holding a practice, a team, and a family, that quiet gets loud. It is easy to start thinking the clients simply are not here.

Here is what we found: you do not have a market problem. You have a visibility problem. And a visibility problem can be fixed.

We took a hard look at how a new client would find you today. The honest answer is that she almost can't. Your current website is working against you:

What a new client runs into right now

  • The main button on your homepage leads to a dead page
  • Your contact form is a clunky embedded Google form that hides its submit button until you scroll
  • Google can barely read your site, so you don't show up in searches
  • Your photo fails to load for most visitors
  • No ads have ever reached the families around you
  • Your practice speaks as "I" while you have a team of three

The plan
One project, five parts

Rebuild the whole path, from first glance to first session.

Each part depends on the one before it. Skipping a step is how marketing money gets wasted. Done together, they become one system that works while you are in session.

1

Get clear

A working session on your brand, your core values, and exactly who you serve best. We put your position in this market into plain words: the message, the person it is for, and why Wildflower over anyone else.
Why it matters: every page, ad, and dollar that follows aims at one person. Without this, marketing sprays and prays.
2

Get a home

A full redesign and build of meetwildflower.com on the direction we have already developed: your watercolor brand, your three therapists, honest fees, and one clear action on every page: request a free 15 minute consult.
Why it matters: every lead ends up here. Right now the site loses them at the door. The new one is built to welcome and convert.
3

Get wired

One-time setup of your ad infrastructure: a Meta business account, your Facebook and Instagram connected, and pixel tracking installed so we can see what every dollar does.
Why it matters: running ads without tracking is spending blind. This wiring makes every future dollar accountable.
4

Get seen

A Meta ad campaign built and published in your Ads Manager: five to ten custom ads, written and designed in your brand, targeted to about a 30 mile radius around your office, and fully compliant with Meta's rules for mental health advertising.
Why it matters: the mom scrolling at 9pm is not searching yet. Ads reach her first, with a message that sounds like you.
5

Get found

A one-time SEO treatment on the new site, setup and optimization of your Google Business Profile, and a tidy lead flow: every call and form lands in one place, so no inquiry slips away.
Why it matters: when someone near Easton searches "therapist near me," the map result gets the call. Today that call goes to someone else.
The point of doing it all at once: clarity shapes the website, the website converts the ad clicks, the pixel proves what works, and Google catches everyone the ads warmed up. One system, pointed at your people.
