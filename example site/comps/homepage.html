Common questions

Answers before you ask.

Are you accepting new clients?

Yes. We see clients ages 10 and up, with an emphasis on pre-teens, teens, and adults — in person in Easton, Maryland, and by telehealth for Maryland and Colorado.

Do you take insurance?

We accept CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield in Maryland for individual therapy. For other plans we're out-of-network and provide a superbill for reimbursement. Co-parenting work is out-of-network for all plans.

How often will we meet?

Most new clients are seen once per week for the first three months or longer — it's how the skills actually take root.
