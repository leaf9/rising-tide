02 · Typography

Cormorant Garamond speaks; Mulish serves

The display serif is light and calligraphic — it rhymes with the logo's brush lettering without imitating it. Mulish carries everything spoken plainly. Italic is the emotional register: reserve it for the phrases that carry faith and feeling.

03 · Shape & space

The brush-cut corner

One shape idea everywhere: an asymmetric radius — sharp on one diagonal, soft on the other — like a brushstroke lifting off paper. Buttons 2/14, cards 4/18, fields 2/10. Pills are the one exception (fully round — a Sea Glass donation). Space runs an 8px scale; sections breathe at --rt-section (64–112px) and body text never exceeds 56ch.

"Request a Free Consult" is the only CTA phrase on the site, and Aqua fill is its only dress. Ghost buttons handle every secondary action.

04 · Water at the seams

Photos carry the seams; paint carries the details

v1.1 (Ben, Aug 27): section seams are carried by real Southwest Florida wave photography faded into the pure-white page (mask-gradient top and bottom — the ocean emerges and recedes). Watercolor moves to accent scale: painted underlines, the footer squiggle, small signature marks — same palette as the wave photos. The containment rules hold for both media: never behind body text; never a hard-edged full-bleed; never more than one water moment on screen at a time.

Code stand-in shown; production bands are generated watercolor (recipes in §07). The painted underline (God's truth and grace) marks faith phrases and key promises — at most one per section.

07 · Imagery program + Higgsfield recipes

Two kinds of images, one palette

Real photography of Jada — seven photos in three settings (assets/jada/): the porch shoot (cream/tan), the palm-tree shoot (golden light), a studio headshot — presented in soft organic masks on Sand tints. The wave library Jada loves (assets/waves/: aerial turquoise wave, sunset shoreline footprints, heart in sand, teal sea on rocks, low-tide ripples) is the primary section-seam imagery — used directly, graded gently (saturate(.85) brightness(1.04)), faded into white with mask gradients. Generated watercolor (Higgsfield, palette-locked) lives at accent scale: assets/art/footer-squiggle.png, assets/art/brush-stroke.png, candidates in assets/art/candidates/. White-background art composites on the white page via mix-blend-mode:multiply — no cutouts needed. Never: generated people, moody dark seas, hard-edged full-bleeds, stock clichés (lighthouses, message bottles, generic laptop shots).

Recipe A — section wave band (≈3:1, hero + seams):

Loose watercolor wave band on white paper, horizontal composition, translucent
overlapping washes of soft aqua (#8FCFC6), pale sky blue (#AED4E4) and sea glass
green (#CFE8E2), wet-on-wet soft edges, subtle granulation, generous untouched
white space above, hand-painted, airy and calm, no text, no objects

Recipe B — brush accents (underlines, dividers, bullet marks):

Single watercolor brushstroke, one confident horizontal sweep, soft aqua

Recipe C — coastal photo look (where a photo band is needed and no library shot fits):

on pale sand, high-key, palette limited to warm sand, soft aqua and sea glass

08 · Voice & UX copy

A counselor's voice, written down

Do

  • Speak as "I" — Jada is a person, not a practice ("I'll respond within one business day")
  • Lead faith-forward, plainly: "from a Biblical worldview," "God's truth and grace"
  • Name feelings before features: "If you're walking through grief…"
  • One CTA phrase everywhere: Request a Free Consult
  • State logistics proudly: $135 / $165, superbills, FL & ME licensure
  • Reassure the nervous first-timer (it's already her best FAQ material)
  • US spelling: counseling, personalized, canceled

Don't

  • "We" — there is no team
  • Clinical jargon walls ("evidence-based modalities for optimal outcomes")
  • Urgency or scarcity tactics — no countdowns, no "only 2 spots left"
  • Vague spirituality ("the universe," "energy") — her faith is specific
  • Multiple competing CTAs or button phrasings
  • Error messages that scold ("Invalid input") — say what helps instead
09 · Standing rules

Non-negotiables downstream

Always

  • Consume --rt-* tokens — zero rogue hex, fonts, radii, timings
  • Washes at seams only, one per viewport, full saturation, contained
  • Crisis note (988) near every contact/consult surface
  • Reserved aspect-ratio slots before any image exists
  • AA contrast minimum — washes never carry text

Never

  • Wash or wave art behind body copy
  • A second filled-button color
  • Generated people or places presented as real
  • Borrowed aesthetics from other Leaf9 projects
  • PHI collected anywhere on this site
