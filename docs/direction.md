## Brand foundation

- **Mandated:** the hand-lettered "Rising Tide · Counseling Services" logo — brush-script
  wordmark in a single flat deep pine-teal, measured **#003130** off the source PNG. The
  lettering has a wave-flourish under "Tide." It is the brand's one finished artifact and
  its brush character is the visual DNA everything else must rhyme with.
- **Given theme (Ben, Aug 27, 2026):** coastal waves — airy, clean, photography-forward,
  watercolor waves.
- **Decided positioning (Ben, Aug 27, 2026):** faith-forward lead. "Clinical Counseling
  from a Biblical Worldview" stays the headline register. The site speaks as "I" (Jada),
  solo practitioner.
- **Values (evidenced in her copy, not invented):** faith-integrated ("God's truth and
  grace"), credentialed depth (dual license FL+ME, 20 years), safety/trust ("safe space"
  appears on every page), whole-person care ("mind, body, and spirit"), hope ("rising,"
  "renewal," "each step brings you closer").
- **Personality:** warm, pastoral, credentialed, gentle — a counselor's hand, not a
  clinic's template. Her real photos (cream/tan, sheer curtains, soft daylight) set the
  temperature: warm and bright, never clinical-cool.

**The central design question:** the tide metaphor can be painted (watercolor), *lit*
(photography), or *distilled* (clean color). All three stay inside Ben's coastal-airy
mandate; they disagree about which medium carries the brand. The concepts answer it
differently.

## Concept 1 — «Watermark»

**Argument.** The only finished brand asset is *paint* — brush-lettered strokes in deep
pine. Watermark lets the brushwork spread beyond the wordmark: translucent watercolor
wave washes in aqua, sky, and sea-glass soak along section seams, the way the flourish
runs under "Tide." On a shell-white ground the page stays airy and clean (honor 2), while
the washes break the sage-beige camouflage (break 1) with something no template has: a
hand. For the woman searching "Christian counselor," watercolor reads gentle, personal,
made-with-care — the visual register of a handwritten note. Photography appears in
soft-edged vignettes inside the washes; scripture-toned pull quotes get painted
underlines. Risk: watercolor executed timidly reads like a spa brochure — the defense is
containment (washes live at seams and edges, never behind body text) and the deep pine
ink keeping every text block crisp AA.

**Ingredients.**
- **Type attitude:** Cormorant Garamond display (light, calligraphic, airy — rhymes with
  brush lettering without imitating it) + Mulish body/UI. Kickers: small caps, wide
  tracking, sea-deep color.
- **Color logic:** shell white `#fdfcf8` ground; brand pine `#003130` ink and sole filled
  CTA; washes aqua `#8fcfc6`, sky `#aed4e4`, sea-glass `#cfe8e2`; warm sand `#ead9bd`
  grounding band near footer. Wash colors are decorative-only; text sits on white or pine.
- **Imagery treatment:** generated watercolor wave bands (logo-palette brushwork, soft
  edges) as structural elements; Jada's real portraits in organic soft-masked shapes;
  later coastal photography lightly desaturated toward the wash palette. SVG-turbulence
  CSS stand-ins until Phase 5.
- **Layout attitude:** centered airy hero, washes at section boundaries, narrow reading
  measures, hairline pine-10% rules.
- **Motion character:** washes fade-soak in (700ms opacity/blur ease-out); content
  fade-ups 450ms; reduced-motion collapses to opacity.

**Ties to map:** breaks 1, 4, 7 via paint; honors 2, 5. **Proof:** `concepts/watermark.html`

## Concept 2 — «First Light» ★ recommended

**Argument.** Faith-forward is the decided lead, and the faith register already in her
copy is *morning* — renewal, mercy, "each step you take brings you closer." First Light
builds the whole site inside one photographic hour: dawn over the water. Golden-hour
coastal photography — first sun on the sea, shoreline footprints, warm sand — is
literally the same light as her two existing portraits (warm cream/tan, soft daylight),
so the only real photos she owns drop into this world seamlessly (honor 5, break 4). A
literary serif set large gives "Clinical Counseling from a Biblical Worldview" the
confidence of a well-set book page (honor 3, break 7): the statement carries weight
without a single decorative crutch. Watercolor keeps Ben's mandate at brand-signature
scale — the logo, one painted wave rule above the footer, painted underlines on scripture
lines — so the paint stays precious instead of wallpaper. This is the concept where the
positioning, the existing photography, and the tide metaphor all point the same
direction. Risk: warm-dark imagery could drift moody; the defense is keeping ground
tones sand-light and reserving deep tones for ink and water.

**Ingredients.**
- **Type attitude:** Literata display (literary, warm, real optical sizes — reads like a
  loved book, fitting a Bible-anchored practice) + Figtree body/UI (round, friendly,
  plainspoken). Scripture/pull quotes: Literata italic, large, generous leading.
- **Color logic:** warm sand ground `#f7f0e4`; deep ink `#22403c` (pine warmed toward
  charcoal); brand pine `#003130` for the sole filled CTA; dawn gold `#c0873c` as the
  one warm accent (kickers, painted underlines); sea-slate `#4e7a82` for secondary UI.
- **Imagery treatment:** photography-led — dawn sea horizons, shoreline walks, footprints
  at the waterline (the visual her testimonial audience already carries), warm-graded to
  one continuous palette; portraits full-height in the hero. Watercolor only at brand
  signature moments.
- **Layout attitude:** editorial — full-bleed photo bands alternating with narrow text
  columns; logistics (pricing, licensure, FAQs) set like a well-kept ledger; generous
  vertical rhythm.
- **Motion character:** near-still; 800ms crossfades, slow parallax on the hero horizon
  only; reduced-motion = static.

**Ties to map:** honors 2, 3, 5, 8; breaks 1 (sand+dawn, not beige — warmth with intent),
4, 6, 7. **Proof:** `concepts/first-light.html`

## Concept 3 — «Sea Glass»

**Argument.** Her practice is 100% telehealth — the site *is* the waiting room, and it
will be managed in Lovable and visited from phones. Sea Glass designs for that reality:
bright, midday-clear coastal color (frosted seafoam tints, glass-aqua), a single modern
humanist sans at strong weight contrast (break 3 — the one concept that trades serif
solemnity for clear-eyed friendliness), soft-rounded cards like tide-tumbled glass, pill
tags for her 16 specialties, and a sticky mobile consult CTA. Faith stated plainly in
confident modern type reads current rather than traditional — for the 28–45 professional
woman booking therapy from her phone between meetings, this is the version that feels
like it will *answer email fast*. The aerial turquoise-wave image world (like the old
site's one good instinct, `beach.jpg`) carries the color story; portraits sit in big
rounded frames. Risk: cool aqua + sans can drift corporate/dental; the defense is warm
shell-coral micro-accents, her warm portraits large, and generous unhurried spacing.

**Ingredients.**
- **Type attitude:** Albert Sans throughout — 200/300 weights huge for display, 500/600
  small for UI; sentence-case headlines; tightened display tracking.
- **Color logic:** white `#fbfdfd` ground; seafoam tints `#e4f2ee` / `#d3ece6` as section
  and card fills; glass-aqua deep `#2f8b80` working accent; brand pine `#003130` ink and
  filled CTA; shell coral `#dd7a5f` micro-accents only (icons, hover states).
- **Imagery treatment:** aerial and clear-water coastal photography (turquoise, sunlit,
  high-key) in rounded-corner cards; subtle wave-line SVG patterns in tint blocks;
  watercolor confined to the logo.
- **Layout attitude:** card-based, 18px radius, mobile-first, pill specialty tags, sticky
  bottom CTA bar on mobile; airy but snappy.
- **Motion character:** gentle micro-interactions (150–250ms lifts on cards/pills),
  minimal scroll reveals.

**Ties to map:** honors 2, 5, 8, 9; breaks 1, 3, 4, 6. **Proof:** `concepts/sea-glass.html`

## Leaf9 recommendation

**«First Light».** It is the only concept where the decided faith-forward positioning,
the two real photos she owns, and the tide metaphor converge without translation: dawn
*is* her message, her portraits already live in that light, and photography-forward was
Ben's own brief. Watermark is the strongest *brand* play (the logo's brushwork earns it)
and the right choice if Jada wants the site to feel hand-made above all; Sea Glass is the
strongest *product* play for a telehealth-native, phone-first audience. Whichever wins,
Sea Glass donates its pill specialty tags and sticky mobile CTA, and Watermark donates
its painted scripture underlines.
